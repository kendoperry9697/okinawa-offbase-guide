About Okinawa Off-Base Guide

Who writes these guides

I'm [your first name], an Okinawa local who has lived here for [X] years. Over the years I've helped many American neighbors and friends with the things that are confusing when you first arrive: getting appliances to work on 100V, preparing for typhoons, finding the beaches and tours that are actually good with kids.

This site collects that advice in one place, in English, so you don't have to piece it together from Facebook groups.
