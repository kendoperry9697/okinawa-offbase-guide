Hosting Family in Okinawa

Where visitors should stay, how to get their phones online, and what to plan.

PR This guide contains affiliate links. If you book or buy through them, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. I only list things I'd actually point a friend to. Details

Parents or friends flying in from the States? Most visitors stay off base, and where they stay makes a big difference in how much time you spend driving. Here's how to think about it.

Where should visitors stay?

Chatan / American Village

Closest to Kadena and Camp Foster. Walkable restaurants, sunset seawall, easy for short visits and for grandparents who want to see you every day.

Hotels near Chatan →

Onna resort coast

Beach resorts with pools — the "vacation" option. About 30–45 minutes north of Chatan; great for a long weekend together.

Resorts in Onna →

Naha

Near the airport and Kokusai-dori. Good for the first or last night of a trip, or for visitors who don't plan to drive.

Get their phone working before they land

U.S. carrier roaming is often expensive. A Japan travel eSIM is usually cheaper and can be installed before the flight — as long as the phone is unlocked and eSIM-compatible.

Airalo Japan eSIM

Buy and install at home, switch it on after landing. Data-only, so calls go through apps like FaceTime or WhatsApp.

Plan the days

For a first visit, most families do the aquarium, a beach day, and Shuri Castle or Kokusai-dori in Naha.

What to book ahead

Churaumi tickets, Kerama day trips, and Blue Cave tours sell out on holiday weekends.
