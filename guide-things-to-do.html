Best Family Weekends in Okinawa

Aquarium tickets, island day trips, and snorkel tours worth booking ahead.

PR This guide contains affiliate links. If you book or buy through them, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. I only list things I'd actually point a friend to. Details

Weekends are what make an Okinawa tour unforgettable. These are the trips families ask about most — the kind worth booking ahead so you're not stuck at a sold-out ticket window with kids in tow.

Churaumi Aquarium

The classic first-month trip, up on the Motobu Peninsula — about 1.5–2 hours' drive from the Kadena/Foster area. The giant Kuroshio Sea tank with its whale sharks is the highlight. Buying tickets online in advance means skipping the ticket line, and you can combine the day with Kouri Island or Bise's fukugi tree lanes.

Kerama Islands day trip

A short ferry or boat ride from Naha, the Keramas have some of the clearest water in Japan, with sea turtles on many snorkel trips. Tours that include gear and transfers are the easiest way to go with kids.

See Kerama tours →

Blue Cave snorkeling (Cape Maeda)

In Onna Village, close to the bases on the west coast. Guided tours handle the gear and pick the safest entry based on that day's waves — conditions here change fast, and tours are sometimes cancelled for surf. Book a morning slot and keep a backup day.

See Blue Cave tours →

Whale watching (winter)

Humpback whales come to the waters around Okinawa roughly from late December through March. Boats leave from Naha and the Keramas; trips can be choppy, so pack seasickness remedies.
