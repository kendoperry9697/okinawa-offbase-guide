Why this site exists

The official PCS checklists cover orders, housing offices, and vehicle registration. They don't tell you which dehumidifier stops your closet from growing mold, which beach tours are worth it with a toddler, or where your parents should stay when they visit. That local, practical layer is what these guides are for — written by someone who lives with Okinawa's humidity and typhoons every year.
